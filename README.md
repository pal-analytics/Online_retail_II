Online Retail Business Analysis using Oracle SQL

Project Title: Online Retail II  
Data: Taken from Kaggle online reatil II

## Problem
The dataset from a UK-based online retail company contained 541,910 transactions across multiple countries. Like most real-world business data, it was messy — with nulls, invalid records, inconsistent date formats, and duplicates. The challenge was to transform this raw data into actionable insights that drive business decisions.


## Approach
- Data Quality Checks
- Identified nulls, invalid quantities/prices, cancelled invoices, and duplicates
- Standardized inconsistent date formats
- Data Modeling & Cleaning
- Built a clean, analytics-ready retail table
- Added time attributes for trend and seasonality analysis
- Business Analysis with SQL
- Applied aggregations, joins, and window functions
- Generated KPI dashboards and trend reports

## Key Insights & KPIs
1. Business Performance
- Unique Customers: 4,372
- Unique Products: 4,070
- Unique Invoices: 25,900
- Average Order Value: 22.4 → bulk/wholesale purchasing behavior
- Top Customer Revenue: ID 14646 → 280,206.02
Impact: High-value customers drive profitability. Retention strategies should prioritize them.

2. Revenue Trends
- Seasonal fluctuations with peak sales in festive months
- Steady cumulative revenue growth with spikes in high-performing months
Impact: Promotions should be aligned with holiday seasons to maximize revenue.

3. Product Performance
- Revenue concentrated in a small subset of products
- Top Product: PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE → 168,469.6 revenue
Impact: Inventory and marketing should focus on top-performing products.

4. Customer Insights
- A limited group of customers contributes disproportionately to revenue
- Several premium transactions > 1000 highlight high-value purchases
Impact: Loyalty programs and personalized offers can maximize retention and upsell opportunities.

5. Geographic Insights
- United Kingdom: 3,920 customers, 7,308,391.55 revenue
- International markets show growth potential
Impact: Targeted expansion strategies could unlock new revenue streams.

## Business Impact
- Revenue Optimization: Identified seasonal peaks and high-value customers for targeted campaigns
- Inventory Planning: Pinpointed top products to prioritize stock and marketing spend
- Customer Retention: Highlighted repeat and premium buyers for loyalty initiatives
- Market Expansion: Revealed international growth opportunities beyond the UK
